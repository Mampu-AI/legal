Account Deletion

App: Sapot Chat (Mampu AI; registered with Meta as JomChat) · Developer: Mampu AI

This page explains how to request the deletion of your Sapot Chat account and the data associated with it.

How to delete your account

Email support@sapot.ai with the subject line Delete my account, sent from the email address associated with your account.

Please include in the email body:

  • The full name on your account
  • The name of your organization / workspace

We will verify ownership and process the request within 30 days.

Deleting data from Facebook Messenger and Instagram

Our platform (also provided as Mampu AI and registered with Meta as the application JomChat) lets businesses receive and reply to messages from Facebook Messenger and Instagram in one inbox. If you sent a message to a business through Messenger or Instagram and would like the data from those interactions deleted, you can request it in either of these ways:

Email support@sapot.ai with the subject line Data Deletion Request, stating the platform and handle you messaged from (for example, your Facebook or Instagram username) and, where known, the business you contacted.

Alternatively, you may ask the business you contacted to delete your conversation and contact record, which they can do directly from their inbox in our platform.

After we verify your request, we delete the associated personal data — including your messages, contact details, and the profile information obtained from the platform — within 30 days, unless we are required or permitted by law to retain it, and we confirm by email. Data obtained via Facebook Messenger and Instagram is used only to operate the messaging inbox and the automated and human-agent replies, and is never sold.

What gets deleted

  • Your user profile (name, email, avatar)
  • Your personal settings and notification preferences
  • Push notification device tokens registered to your account
  • Authentication credentials and active sessions

What is retained

  • Conversations and messages you participated in. These belong to your organization's workspace, not your personal account, and are retained for the workspace owner's records and legal/audit purposes.
  • System logs are retained for up to 90 days for security and audit.
  • Anonymized analytics (no personal identifiers) may be retained indefinitely.

If your organization administers your account

If your Sapot Chat account was created for you by an administrator (for example, your employer's IT team), please also notify them. Your organization may have additional retention obligations for workplace records.
